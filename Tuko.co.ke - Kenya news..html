The Spanish Ministry of Education and Vocational Training has opened its 2026–2027 scholarship cycle, offering financial support to eligible students and researchers across undergraduate, master's, doctoral and postdoctoral levels at accredited Spanish institutions.

The programmes, collectively known as the Spanish Government Scholarships, are primarily available to Spanish nationals, with select schemes also open to EU citizens holding Spanish residency.

All applicants must be enrolled or admitted to an official Spanish higher education institution and must satisfy each programme's minimum academic standards.

Scholarship programmes and benefits

Several distinct programmes fall under the 2026–2027 cycle. The Becas MEC (Ministerio de Educación y Formación Profesional) covers undergraduate, master's and vocational training students through tuition fee waivers, income-assessed grants and supplementary allowances.

The General State Scholarships (Becas Generales del Estado) similarly address the cost of studying at Spanish universities and officially recognised institutions.

For researchers, two postdoctoral fellowships are on offer: the Juan de la Cierva contracts, targeting early-stage researchers, and the Ramón y Cajal fellowships, designed for experienced researchers pursuing advanced work.

Additionally, foundation-backed awards from organisations such as Fundación La Caixa and Fundación Rafael del Pino support postgraduate and doctoral candidates.

Depending on the specific programme, awards may cover full or partial tuition fees, monthly living allowances, accommodation, meals or transport subsidies, special provisions for students with disabilities, and mobility funding for Master's and PhD candidates.

Eligible subjects and application process

The scholarships span a wide range of academic disciplines, including humanities and arts, engineering and technology, natural and life sciences, social sciences and law, health and medicine, education and vocational training.

Prospective applicants are advised to access the official Spanish Ministry of Education Scholarships Portal, identify the programme corresponding to their degree level, and submit an online application with supporting documents.

KRA Opens Applications for 2027 Internship Programme, Lists Requirements

Published 24 Nov 2026 at 7:25 AM
by  Elijah Ntongai
3 min read

Search option is now available at TUKO! Feel free to search the content on topics/people you enjoy reading about in the top right corner ;)

  • The Kenya Revenue Authority opened applications for its 2027 internship programme targeting recent bachelor's degree and diploma graduates
  • KRA listed over 50 eligible disciplines and set a December 14, 2026 deadline for all online applications
  • Successful interns will earn a monthly stipend of KSh 25,000 for degree holders and KSh 18,000 for diploma holders

The Kenya Revenue Authority (KRA) has opened applications for its January to December 2027 Internship Programme, inviting recent graduates to apply for one-year placements across the organisation.

The authority is accepting applications from individuals who hold a Bachelor's Degree or Diploma from a recognised institution, with graduation required to have occurred between January 2024 and December 2026.

Applicants must also be available on a full-time basis throughout the twelve-month duration of the programme and must not have previously undertaken any other internship.

Eligible disciplines and stipend

KRA has opened the programme across a broad range of fields, including finance, accounting, economics, data analytics, ICT, law, engineering, journalism, human resource management, supply chain management, counselling psychology, and actuarial science, among more than 50 listed disciplines.

Read also

Public Service Commission opens applications for additional 2026/2027 internship positions

Degree holders selected for the programme will receive a monthly stipend of KSh 25,000, while diploma holders will receive KSh 18,000.

Both amounts are subject to statutory deductions. KRA clarified that the programme does not guarantee permanent employment upon completion.

How to apply

All applications must be submitted through KRA's e-recruitment portal at erecruitment.kra.go.ke.

Candidates are required to register on the portal, complete their profile under the Candidate Profile section, and then navigate to Employment Opportunities to locate and submit the internship application.

Incomplete applications will not be considered, and only shortlisted candidates will be contacted.

Once selected, successful candidates will be required to present a valid personal accident insurance cover, a certificate of good conduct, copies of their KRA PIN certificate, NHIF/SHIF card, NSSF card, a national identity card, and bank account details.

The deadline for applications is Monday, December 14, 2026. KRA also confirmed that it does not charge any fee for the application process and that canvassing will result in automatic disqualification.

The authority noted that persons with disabilities are encouraged to apply, in line with its equal opportunity employment policy.

Tips for applicants

Considering the unemployment rate in the country, thousands of graduates apply for the annual internship programme.

To stand out during applications, career expert Emmanuel Makokha advised applicants to explain their achievements, projects and key responsibilities they have undertaken.

"Even if you lack formal experience. Focus on highlighting your academic achievements, relevant coursework, and any projects or assignments that demonstrate your skills. Include extracurricular activities, volunteer work, and leadership roles that show initiative and teamwork," he explained.

PSC opens additional 2026/2027 government internship applications

In other news, the Public Service Commission (PSC) has opened applications for additional internship positions across government ministries, state departments and agencies under the expanded National Internship Programme (NIP) for the 2026/2027 financial year.

Applicants must hold at least a diploma or bachelor's degree from a recognised institution, demonstrate computer proficiency and must not have previously benefited from a comparable government internship programme.

Successful candidates will undertake non-renewable 12-month placements in public institutions, receive a monthly stipend at a government-determined rate and earn a PSC certificate upon completion.

Applications must be submitted through the PSC recruitment portals, www.publicservice.go.ke or www.psckjobs.go.ke, by December 14, 2026.
