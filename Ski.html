We have our fingers crossed that El Niño delivers. Of course, forecasting is a fickle business, and we’ve had our hearts broken by overhyped winters before. We’ll believe it when we ski it. However, one thing is guaranteed in the forecast: Black Friday deals. An atmospheric river of sales is coming our way, and there is a 100 percent chance it’ll dump deals between now and Cyber Monday.

All jokes aside, now is the time to stock up on ski gear, so you’re ready to get after it when powder days materialize in the (hopefully) not-so-distant future. To help you sift through the mountains of discounted gear online during the Black Friday insanity, we’re sharing 13 sales worth checking out right now, in addition to the always-on-and-updated SKI Deals page. Below, you’ll find bargains from heavyweight retailers like REI, Backcountry, and Evo as well as brand-specific sales at Arc’teryx, Scott, Rossignol, and more.

Over 50% Off At Evo

Shop Now

Evo’s having a massive Black Friday sale, with over 10,000 products included in the main promotion, and over 3,000 of those products are ski-related. Evo’s one of our favorite corners of the internet if we’re on the hunt for discounted hard goods, and that’s especially true right now. If you need skis, boots, or bindings, this is the place to be. And Evo is overflowing with outerwear and accessories, too!

A few standout deals from the Evo clearance department that our gear team flagged: $370 off last season’s El Niño-ready Armada Whitewalker 121s (only a few pairs left!), Evo’s in-house Deluxe Snow Roller Bag for under $200, and up to half off Oyuki’s Hokkaido-proven ski jackets and pants

REI’s Holiday Sale

Shop Now

REI has a bunch of promotions leading up to Black Friday and running through Cyber Week, including outlet flash sales and special discount codes for Co-op members. The best way to stay up to speed? Visit REI’s deals page here. Or skip it and head straight to these ski-specific sales.

Up to 60% Off at Backcountry’s Black Friday Sale

Shop Now

Backcountry’s going all out for Black Friday. You can peep the full Backcountry Black Friday sale here, which is overflowing with gear up to 60 percent off. It’s also worth noting that Backcountry shoppers can score 20 percent off eligible full-price gear using code at checkout!

Up to 60% Off at Sun & Ski

Shop Now

Don’t sleep on Sun & Ski. The online outfitter is liquidating inventory left and right during its Black Friday sale. A few deals that made our admittedly jaded gear nerds (they’ve seen it all at this point!) double take:

  • 43 percent off Anon’s M4 Toric goggles. Stellar optics, the fastest lens interchangeability on the market, and a magnetic facemask make this a lovely gift for any skier, especially at the Black Friday pricing.
  • Atomic Bent 100s for $500… and no, they’re not used.
  • 20 percent off Thule’s dependable, carry-on-sized 35L RoundTrip Boot Bag.

Arc’teryx: Extra 20% Off Outlet Items and Up to 40% Off

Shop Now

Arc’teryx is notorious for its premium pricing, but you generally get what you pay for. Thankfully, you get a lot more than you pay for during the outdoor juggernaut’s Black Friday sale. Arc’teryx loyalists can score an extra 20 percent off already-discounted outlet items, and up to 40 percent off current gear and apparel this week.

Up to 50% Off Rossignol

Shop Now

Rossi might need to dial up its DINs, because this gear is half-off! Get it? Bad single-ejection jokes aside, you can scoop up Rossignol outerwear, goggles, and more for up to 50 percent off during the French outfitter’s sizable Black Friday sale.

20% Off Rocky Talkies

Shop Now

Solid comms are a critical piece of any backcountry skier’s kit. Rocky Talkie’s compact, effective, and intuitive radios are becoming ubiquitous on the skintrack, and we’re here for it. Our advice? Grab one Rocky Talkie radio for yourself, and gift another to your go-to backcountry buddy. They’re 20 percent off right now, and this is arguably the most essential piece of gear in this entire Black Friday round-up.

Up to 50% Off Scott Apparel

Shop Now

If the El Niño hype train delivers, you’ll need outerwear that can withstand an atmospheric onslaught. Luckily, Scott is graciously slanging its alpine armor for up to 50 percent off during its Black Friday sale. Sizing is limited for the deepest discounts, so we’d straightline over now if you’re interested in new duds for the deepest days.

Up to 25% Off Black Diamond

Shop Now

Whether you need heavy-duty leather paw protectors (we love ourselves a set of Guide Gloves), a puffy that can handle sub-zero winter camping missions, or a rechargeable headlamp (always a sweet stocking stuffer!), this Black (Diamond) Friday sale is worth a gander. You won’t find the most extreme discounts on this one, but you will find gear that’s built to withstand the most extreme conditions.

4 Black Friday Recovery Deals for Post-Shred Bliss

Haven’t you heard? Recovery is the new après. These four sales are packed with deals on compression sleeves, next-gen massagers, and everything you need to recover after bouncing through mogul fields like it was 1999. We even threw in a massive deal on saunas and cold plunges, because, well, what beats a post-pow-day schvitz?

Save Hundreds on Normatec Compression

Shop Now

You’ve probably seen your favorite skier posted up at PT with a set of Normatec compression sleeves wrapped around their quads like a pair of black boa constrictors trying to swallow a couple of Christmas hams. We can vouch that the tech works like a charm, but it costs an arm and a leg to bring Normatec compression into the comfort of your own home. During Hyperice’s Black Friday sale, you can save hundreds of bucks on Normatec compression tech, and snag heavy discounts on Hyperice massage guns, contrast therapy essentials, and more.

$90 Off Rally Orbital Massager

Shop Now

Rally’s Orbital Massager will revolutionize your recovery. Unlike massage guns that jackhammer in and out, Rally’s Orbital Massager makes circular rotations, too, offering what we’ve found to be deeper and more impactful muscular relief. The innovative recovery tool costs a pretty penny, but Black Friday buyers can save $90 this week.

Plunge’s Biggest Sale Ever: Up to 30% Off

Shop Now

Virtually every skier dreams of a backyard sauna. Icy cold plunges, on the other hand, might be more of a nightmare—depends on who you ask. Plunge is making it easier than ever to turn backyard recovery dreams into reality with its biggest sale in history. You can score 25 percent off saunas and 30 percent off cold plunges this week. Granted, these Plunge luxuries are by no means cheap, but they are currently cheaper than usual!

$100 Off Chirp Contour Decompression and Massage Table

Shop Now

If your lower back feels like it needs a massage after every day on the hill, Chirp’s Contour Decompression and Massage Table turns your living room floor into a spa. The Contour combines warming heat and a rolling massage mechanism to generate genuine relief for aching backs. It doesn’t come close to replacing the expertise of a trained bodyworker, but it’s a welcome addition to your après routine.
