General Staff reports 161 combat clashes on front line over past day

24.11.2026 09:05
Ukrinform
Listen to audio version of this article
Sponsored by
0:00 / 2:06
Instaread
A total of 161 combat engagements between the Ukrainian Defense Forces and Russian troops were recorded along the front line on November 23.

This was reported by the General Staff of the Armed Forces of Ukraine in a Facebook post, according to Ukrinform.

Russian forces carried out 73 airstrikes, dropping 250 guided aerial bombs. They also deployed 7,475 kamikaze drones and launched 1,865 attacks, including 17 using multiple-launch rocket systems.

The Russian strikes targeted multiple settlements in the Sumy region.

The Ukrainian Defense Forces' aviation, missile forces and artillery struck two clusters of Russian personnel and military equipment, one UAV command post, and two artillery systems.

In the Northern Slobozhanshchyna and Kursk sectors, Russian forces launched three attacks, carried out five strikes with 11 guided aerial bombs, and conducted 106 shelling attacks, six of them using multiple-launch rocket systems.

In the Southern Slobozhanshchyna sector, Ukrainian troops repelled one Russian attack near Okhrimivka.

In the Kupiansk sector, Russian forces did not conduct any assault operations.

In the Lyman sector, Russian troops launched four attacks near Zelena Dolyna and Zarichne, as well as toward Lyman.

In the Sloviansk sector, Russian forces carried out four attacks near Dibrova and Yampil.

In the Kramatorsk sector, Russian troops did not conduct any assault operations.

In the Kostiantynivka sector, 18 Russian attacks were recorded. Russian forces conducted offensive operations near Kostiantynivka, Dovha Balka, and Vilne, as well as toward Osikove, Oleksiievo-Druzhkivka, Mykolaipillia, and Kucheriv Yar.

Russian forces launched 15 attacks in the Pokrovsk sector, attempting to advance near Chernihivka, Dobropillia, Novohryhorivka, Vasylivka, Myrne, and Molodetske, as well as toward Serhiivka.

In the Oleksandrivka sector, Russian forces carried out one assault toward Kalynivske.

In the Huliaipole sector, Russian troops launched 13 attacks, attempting to advance toward Yehorivka, Nove Zaporizhzhia, and Rizdvianka, as well as near Dobropillia, Vozdvyzhivka, Huliaipilske, and Charivne.

In the Orikhiv and Dnipro River sectors, Russian forces did not conduct any assault operations.

No signs of Russian forces forming offensive groupings were detected in the Volyn and Polissia sectors.

As Ukrinform reported, Russia's total estimated combat losses since the start of its full-scale invasion of Ukraine on February 24, 2022, had reached approximately 1,641,489 personnel as of November 24, 2026.

Germany to become first country granted access to Ukraine’s DELTA combat system

photo
24.11.2026 08:50
Ukrinform
Ukrainian Defense Minister Yevhenii Khmara and German Defense Minister Boris Pistorius have signed a letter of intent in Berlin on cooperation in the development, deployment and use of the DELTA combat digital ecosystem.

The Ukrainian Ministry of Defense announced this in a statement, Ukrinform reports.

The agreement marks a new stage in expanding Ukrainian defense technologies under the DELTA Lab initiative.

Germany will be able to use DELTA in line with the needs of its armed forces, adapt its capabilities, contribute to the development of new features and work jointly to further develop the system.

As part of the cooperation, Germany plans to support DELTA's further development, including through the IT Coalition for Ukraine. At the same time, its use by the German side will not involve the transfer of, or access to, combat or other operational data belonging to the Ukrainian Defense Forces.

"Ukraine has technological solutions that have been tested on the battlefield. DELTA is a unique digital platform that brings together planning, operational command and situational analysis. German technological expertise will help develop and scale up the system's capabilities. This is an important step in our mutually beneficial defense cooperation. The partnership between Ukraine and Germany will strengthen both our countries and security across Europe," Khmara said.

The next step will be the signing of a separate agreement setting out the practical, technical and legal terms of the cooperation.

DELTA is a combat digital ecosystem developed by the Ministry of Defense of Ukraine's Center for Innovation and Development of Defense Technologies. The system integrates tools for situational awareness, intelligence, planning and combat mission management.

Read also: Ukraine, Germany to launch joint production of unmanned ground systems – Pistorius

As Ukrinform reported, Pistorius previously said that the German Army had tested Ukraine's DELTA digital battlefield management and situational awareness system in October during exercises involving advanced drone technologies. Germany now plans to work with Ukraine to further develop the system and assess its potential use by the Bundeswehr.
