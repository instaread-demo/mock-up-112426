Tshituka in line for Springbok debut in Nations Championship final

Emmanuel Tshituka is in line to make his Springbok debut when South Africa face France in the Nations Championship final in London on Sunday.

Springbok head coach Rassie Erasmus has made seven changes and one positional switch to the starting team that defeated Ireland last weekend.

Damian Willemse misses out after picking up a niggle in Dublin, with Manie Libbok moving to flyhalf and Sacha Feinberg-Mngomezulu shifting to fullback.

Ruan Nortje has also failed to recover from injury, paving the way for Salmaan Moerat to start alongside Lood de Jager in the second row.

There are three changes in the front row, with Jan-Hendrik Wessels, Gerhard Steenekamp and Thomas du Toit coming into the starting XV.

Tshituka, the brother of Springbok loose forward Vincent Tshituka, is named among the replacements and could earn his first Test cap if he takes the field.

Erasmus has again opted for a six-two split on the bench, with Malcom Marx, Ox Nche and Wilco Louw providing front-row cover, while Eben Etzebeth covers lock.

Tshituka and Paul de Villiers provide loose-forward cover, with Morne van den Berg and Canan Moodie the two backs on the bench.

Erasmus says Tshituka has earned his opportunity after working hard at training.

“We are proud of him to earn this fantastic opportunity in a final, especially after waiting patiently for his chance, and this is a big occasion for him, so we know he will give everything on the field.”

South Africa will be looking to bounce back after defeats in their last two matches, with Erasmus stressing that there will be little room for error against a dangerous French side.

France beat the Springboks in Paris two weeks ago and will enter the final with confidence, but Erasmus says his team knows exactly what is required.

The match kicks off at 18:40 South African time on Sunday and will be broadcast live on SuperSport.
