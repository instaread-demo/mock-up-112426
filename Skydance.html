'Yellowjackets' Returns Home for Its Final Season

Melanie Lynskey, Christina Ricci and their Yellowjackets co-stars reflect on saying goodbye, lasting friendships and the final season’s homecoming.

After years of surviving the wilderness and its aftermath, the Yellowjackets are headed back where it all began, and the cast is bracing for a homecoming that also marks a goodbye.

The fourth and final season of Yellowjackets, premiering November 20 on the Paramount+ Premium Plan, brings the survivors home to the world they left behind. But rescue means confronting what happened in the woods and figuring out how to live with it.

Created by Ashley Lyle and Bart Nickerson, Yellowjackets has followed a high school girls’ soccer team’s fight for survival after a plane crash, alongside the lives they’ve attempted to piece together nearly 25 years later. Its blend of psychological horror, survival epic and coming-of-age drama reached a record audience in season three, the show’s most-watched season, with the April 2025 finale drawing 3 million global viewers over seven days on Paramount+ to become its most-streamed episode ever.

To promote the final season, Paramount+ imagined how the survivors’ rescue would have entered the pop culture conversation in the ’90s. Social teasers used familiar MTV formats to tell that fictional news: an MTV News segment “reported” the team’s return, while an Alanis Morissette MTV Unplugged performance of “Ironic” incorporated an acknowledgment of their rescue. The campaign invited fans to experience the homecoming through the world the teenagers were returning to.

For the teenage survivors, homecoming marks a dramatic shift. After murder, cannibalism and the rise of an Antler Queen, they must return to high school life. That transition also transformed the actors’ experience, taking them out of the wilderness and into scenes with families, classmates and a world beyond the team. “It felt like a completely different show,” Sophie Nélisse, who plays teen Shauna, told The Hollywood Reporter.

Jasmin Savoy Brown, who plays teen Taissa, described how different that felt, right down to their appearance. “It was weird. Even just physically, we were used to being dirty and nasty and we’re suddenly clean and we were filming with other actors for the first time in seven years; we had only filmed with each other,” she said. For Sophie Thatcher, who plays teen Natalie, trading the woods for high school hallways left her missing the wilderness: “I will take the wilderness any day. Cover me in dirt.”

Jenna Burgess as Teen Melissa, Nia Sondaya as Teen Akilah, Liv Hewson as Teen Van and Sophie Nélisse as Teen Shauna in Yellowjackets.

Offscreen, saying goodbye means celebrating the relationships built along the way. “We have all been on such a journey together,” says Melanie Lynskey. “What a privilege to get to work with these women and I hope people like the final season. I feel very lucky.”

Tawny Cypress as Taissa and Melanie Lynskey as Shauna in Yellowjackets.

Christina Ricci describes a bond rooted in that shared experience. “We are all very close. We have all been through this whole thing together and we're all very similar ages,” she says. For Tawny Cypress, those relationships will endure beyond the series. “I loved spending time with these girls,” she says. “They became some of my dearest friends.”

Christina Ricci as Misty in Yellowjackets.

Those friendships are part of what the cast takes away from Yellowjackets. The final season also offers something Samantha Hanratty, who plays teen Misty, is grateful to share with viewers: a chance to see the story through. “Well, we got to have our start, our middle, our end, and I’m really excited for everybody to see,” she told Deadline.

Samantha Hanratty as Teen Misty in Yellowjackets.

The final season of Yellowjackets premieres November 20 on the Paramount+ Premium Plan.
