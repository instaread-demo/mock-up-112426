GOP Leaders Open to Cutting Off Iran War Funding

November 23, 2026 · 6:26 pm ET · 29 Comments

Listen to audio version of this article
Sponsored by
0:00 / 0:45
Instaread

“Senate Republicans hoping to avoid a government shutdown are considering a deal with Democrats that would cut off funding for the Iran war, an unpopular conflict that Congress never approved and that contributed to the GOP’s crushing midterm losses,” the Washington Post reports.

“As Congress prepared last week to leave town for Thanksgiving, Senate Appropriations Committee Chairwoman Susan Collins (R-ME) told reporters that she is exploring the possibility of a deal to devote fresh cash to restocking dangerously depleted munitions at the Pentagon while denying the Trump administration funds specifically for continuing the Iran conflict.”
