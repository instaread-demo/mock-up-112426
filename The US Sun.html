T.J. Maxx and two more major retailers set to move into new 92,600sqft shopping complex

MULTIPLE retailers, including TJ Maxx, are moving into a new US shopping complex.

The news comes as TJ Maxx continues to reshape its retail footprint, losing stores under its own banner but seeing growth through its sister chains.

But TJ Maxx is getting all the glory here, alongside Academy Sports + Outdoors and PetSmart.

All three retailers are opening at the new Headwaters Retail shopping center project in Dripping Springs, Texas.

According to filings with the Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation (TDLR), the shopping center project will span more than 30 acres.

Five separate filings were made to the TDLR last week that make up the whole project, and the three retailers were included in the largest filing.

The filing calls for a roughly 92,600-square-foot retail building, with work expected to total $7.4 million.

Four smaller buildings would add nearly 39,000 square feet for other tenants, which have not been named.

Construction on the project, led by Endeavor Real Estate Group, is expected to begin on December 7  and be complete by September 1, 2028.

The announcement comes as welcome relief for PetSmart, which has confirmed store closures in recent months.
