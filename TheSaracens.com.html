Saracens v Harlequins Premiership Cup Report


License to score
IMAGO/Uk Sports Pics Ltd

By saracensfan62
November 21 2026

Listen to audio version of this article
Sponsored by
0:00 / 4:17
Instaread

The Premiership Cup is fast becoming a real silverware prospect for us this season! Another bumper win against Harlequins, although it did not come without the odd blip or two. The rest of the Saracens-supporting family went along to watch this one and their comments were, for the most part, positive. They were not a fan of the music being blasted at every interval nor Saracens rather slow start, but either way they were impressed by Nathan Michelow and the youngsters. 

First Half

Harlequins were by far the faster side out of the blocks, scoring 2 tries in a matter of minutes. The first came from a delightful dummy from Luke Northmore, completing wrong-footing Nick Tompkins and allowing him to glide through the line. He managed to find Jamie laing who scuttled over the line, just holding Noah Caluori off for long enough to make it. 0-5 within 8 minutes. Quins enjoyed some possession and looked handy in open play, it wasn’t  long till they got the next score. Another flowing backs mov allowed winger Cam Anderson to make a break. Slick handling from centre Bryn Bradley allowed Lucas Friday to dot down, plus the conversion was added. An ugly 0-12 scoreline 13 mins in and it wasn’t looking to good for the Wolfpack.

However we responded with defiance, Alfie Barbeary dotting down after sustained forwards pressure and a catch-n-drive maul almost went awry. Faz converted and it was 7-12. To make things better, we got another a mere 4minutes after. A lovely line break from Nathan Michelow, starting with a James Hadfield offload, meant the ball was eventually worked out to Sione Va’enuku who bulldozed over Nick David to dot down. Farrel was spot on with the conversion and it was 14-12 to us now.
We were starting to really profit off some good territory, and it wasn’t long until Barbeary got his brace. An overthrown James Hadfield lineout on the Quins 5m found Nathan Michelow, who almost barnstormed the way over himself. However there was 0 doubt that when Barbeary picked and went, the try wouldn’t be scored. 30 minutes gone and the game ws turned on its head – 21-12 to Saracens.

On 37minutes, things only got better. Another try courtesy of a delightful crossfield kick from Owen Farrell to Noah Caluori. Some individual brilliance from Nathan Michelow, spotting space where there seemed to be none, and showing an excellent turn of speed to outpace the quins backs to the line. 33-12 the halftime score and what a turnaround the 20-40 minute period had been!

Second Half

A mere 2 mins in and we looked to be having the same, slow start in the 2nd half that happened in the 1st. Bryn Bradley found soft shoulders a couple of metres out from the sarries line and went over to score. Harlequins enjoyed some sustained pressure in-between the 45-55 or so minute mark, however couldn’t convert. It was after they won a penalty on their own 22 for Sarries holding on, Lucas Friday found a way to capitalise. He took a quick tap, and hoofed the ball deep into the sarries 22. What should’ve been an ordinary gather for Tobias Elliott was made awkward by the onrushing Quins SH and forced an error, Friday grabbing the ball for himself and offloading to the support runner Nick David. Suddenly It was 33-26 and a Lot closer than we would have wanted.

However as the old adage goes – “Anything you can do, I can do better” and Tompkins did to Luke Northmore what Northmore did to him in the 1st half. He threw a lovely dummy and made a missed pass to Roti Segun who needed no 2nd invitation to hurtle through to the line. 38-26 and we had some breathing space again.

Sarries confirmed the win on 78 minutes after a delightful Max Malins grubber kick bounce perfectly for Daly to collect and dot down with precision in the corner. 43-26 the final score and a good win.

Plenty of areas to work on (the lineout in parts, defence a recurring theme) but it always feels grand to win against Quins, irrespective of the competition.

Come on you Sarries!

by Taboolaby Taboola
