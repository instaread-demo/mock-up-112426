New York Burglaries Down But Still Every 22 Minutes

Glenn PitcherPublished: November 24, 2026
  • Share on Facebook
  • Share on Twitter

New York State had the 13th lowest reported burglary rate in the country last year, according to analysis of FBI crime data. That is good news, but reported burglaries still happened every 22 minutes across the state in 2025.

New York Reported Nearly 24,000 Burglaries Last Year

The analysis from Uplift Legal Funding found that 23,856 burglaries were reported in New York in 2025. That is 121 reported burglaries for every 100,000 residents covered by agencies that report crime data to the FBI. New York’s rate was 38% below the national average.

The Typical Reported Loss Was $730

In reported New York burglaries where the amount stolen was known, the median value of stolen property was $730. That was lower than the national median of $1,047. However, the cost of a burglary goes beyond what was taken. Victims may have to repair broken doors or windows and deal with the fear of having their home violated.

Buffalo Had a Higher Than Average Burglary Rate

The Buffalo Cheektowaga metro area had 2,247 reported burglaries in 2025. Its rate was 212.5 burglaries per 100,000 covered residents, which was 9.1% above the national average. The report found that a burglary was reported in the Buffalo area about every three hours and 54 minutes.

Rochester and New York City Had Lower Rates

Rochester had a reported burglary rate of 158.3 per 100,000 covered residents. The New York City metro area had a rate of 126 per 100,000 covered residents. Those metro areas were not included in the national large metro area ranking because less than 80% of residents were covered by agencies reporting to the FBI’s system.

Simple Home Security Steps Can Help

Burglars look for an easy chance to get inside. Locking doors and windows, using outdoor lighting can help make a home less appealing to a criminal. Home alarms and cameras can also discourage burglaries and help police during an investigation if a break in happens.

Top 10 Items Stolen in Home Invasions

Data from FBI findings.

Gallery Credit: Kolby Fedore, Townsquare Media

Canva

Cash

The number one thing stolen during a home invasion, per FBI findings, is cash. Common hiding places where people keep cash include sock drawers, refrigerators, under mattresses, or behind picture frames.

Designer Clothes

Thieves might be interested in designer clothes, jewelry, shoes, handbags and other accessories to resell. A London gang recently tunneled into a luxury designer's boutique through a wall to steal over £250,000 in luxury handbags!

Canva

Prescription Drugs

Stealing prescription drugs can be extremely profitable for home burglars. Medications are easily resold on the streets, which is why many home burglars target the medicine cabinet in your bathroom during a break-in. Some over-the-counter medications might also get stolen in the process. A burglar probably won’t stop to read the label, so if you keep things like painkillers, allergy medicine and even vitamins on hand, a burglar might even decide to take it.

Canva

Cars and Car Parts

Burglars may see your car as an easier target than your home. And stealing the car itself may not even be the goal. A band of burglars could be interested in swiping your catalytic converter. In the first six months of 2023, nearly 27,000 catalytic converters were reported stolen nationwide. A burglar could also smash your windshield in order to access items in your glove box. According to the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA), burglars may also be after everything from your wheel covers and transmission to your GPS unit.

Canva

Electronics

Electronics, like televisions, computer sets and gaming systems are common targets for burglars. Nonetheless, your phone may also be a targeted item. A study found that mobile devices are the most commonly stolen devices, at about a 73 percent rate compared to laptops and desktops, at 20 and 6 percent, respectively.

Canva

Jewelry

Fine jewelry and watches are often stolen for resell value to turn a quick profit. If you have jewelry or watches lying out in plain sight, it’s fair game for potential theft.

Canva

Personal Documents

Some burglars are after personal documents like your Social Security card, password books, or passport. If stolen, burglars can use these items to steal your identity and get into financial accounts. The U.S. Federal Trade Commission (FTC) received 1.4 million reports of identity theft in 2024 alone, highlighting how frequent this serious crime takes place.

Canva

Firearms

Firearms are one of the most stolen items in home invasions. According to data from the Bureau of Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms and Explosives (ATF), more than 95 percent of guns originate from thefts from private citizens (not specific to home invasions). Since many guns have an extremely high resale value, burglars often target them to quickly resell on the streets.

Canva

Power Tools

Like jewelry and firearms, power tools are often nabbed for resell. One way to prevent theft is by locking your tools in a shed, some go even further by engraving tools to deter thieves.

Canva

Liquor

While burglars often prioritize easily resold items like electronics, jewelry, and cash, some individuals might also steal bottles of liquor they find in a home. This is more likely to happen if the burglar has a personal interest in alcohol or believes they can easily resell it.

Six Vehicle Modifications That Are Illegal in New York

If you live in New York, before you make any modifications to your vehicle, you might want to check out the six modifications that are illegal in the state.

Gallery Credit: Traci Taylor

Canva

Vehicle Frame and Suspension

Believe it or not, there are no regulations in New York when it comes to suspension and frame lift height. However, all cars and SUVs are required to have bumpers mounted between 16 and 20 inches. In New York, trucks have a maximum bumper height of 30 inches.

Canva

Vehicle Sound System

Love a good thump? That's cool but just don't go overboard in New York because here, sound that is 15 or more decibels above the ambient sound in a given area when measured 15 or more feet from the source is not allowed.

Canva

Vehicle Window Tint

Non-reflective tint is allowed but only on the top six inches of the windshield. Rear windows can have any tint darkness but your front, back, and side windows are required to allow in over 70 percent of light. If your back window is tinted, side mirrors are absolutely required.

Canva

Vehicle Mufflers

Loud muffler? You can definitely get pulled over if its sound level is higher than 15 decibels more than the ambient sound of the vehicle being driven. On that note, muffler cutouts are illegal in New York.

Canva

Vehicle Lights

If you drive an emergency vehicle, you can have red and blue flashing lights on it. If you do not drive an emergency vehicke, you cannot. Also, the only vehicle lights allowed are ones installed at the factory which means you cannot have auxiliary lights on your vehicle.

Canva

Vehicle Engines

New York doesn't have any regulations on engine swaps or modifications but keep in mind that any vehicle registered in New York is required to undergo testing and safety inspections every year.
