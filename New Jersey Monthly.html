Local Makers Are Cooking Up Holiday Catering and Edible Gifts

Rising Kitchens is a North Jersey-based business that connects emerging chefs and food entrepreneurs with customers. The holidays are a great time to order from its partners.

by Georgia Kral November 23, 2026

A holiday cookie tin from Red Wolfe PatisserieCourtesy of Rising Kitchens

Back in 2023, Lauren Silverstein heard about a busboy at a restaurant whose mother made tamales so delicious that he would bring them into work to give to coworkers. They loved them so much that they started placing orders.

“My first thought was, I bet a lot of people would want this busboy’s mom’s tamales,” says Silverstein, who lives in Morristown. “It got me thinking about all the talent out there.”

She realized there was a growing need to connect emerging food makers with customers, and soon Rising Kitchens—a “connection hub” for culinary talent—was born.

Rising Kitchens partners with burgeoning chefs, bakers and entrepreneurs in North Jersey to provide opportunities for catering and bulk food orders. It also assists with business development, from cost calculations to food photography. Each year’s cohort includes around 10 chefs, and the process is selective.

“We accept new chefs once a year, and the product has to be really good. We interview the chefs and taste the food,” Silverstein says. “It’s our reputation.”

All makers devise their menus and cook their products in commercial kitchens, as required by law. Many operate out of the Orange-based incubator Garden State Kitchen—which was recently purchased by one of the Rising Kitchens partners, Silverstein says.

The holidays are a great time to dip into the growing landscape of local makers’ products and services. Here are a few options:

  • Stress-free holiday catering from The Culinary Curate
  • Holiday cookie tins from Red Wolfe Patisserie
  • Customizable, individually wrapped treats from Cereal Dreams
  • Chocolates topped with dried fruits or edible flowers from Tea Time Chocolates
