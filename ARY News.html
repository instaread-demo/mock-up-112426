Pakistan set to make history as first astronaut joins China's Shenzhou-24 Mission

  • By Sheeraz Soomro
    • -
  • Nov 24, 2026

Pakistan is set to achieve a major milestone in space exploration as Pakistani astronaut Muhammad Zeeshan Ali prepares to travel aboard China’s Shenzhou-24 space mission, becoming the first Pakistani astronaut selected to visit China’s Tiangong Space Station.

Speaking during a media briefing alongside fellow astronauts participating in the mission, Muhammad Zeeshan Ali said his participation would represent not only himself but the entire nation of Pakistan.

“I am going into space carrying the hopes and aspirations of 240 million Pakistanis,” he said, expressing pride in representing his country on an international scientific mission.

Highlighting the strong bilateral relationship between Pakistan and China, Ali said the friendship between the two countries was deeper than the oceans and higher than the Himalayas. He added that the mission would open a new chapter in Pakistan-China space cooperation.

The Pakistani astronaut also revealed that he had learned the Chinese language and developed close, brotherly relations with Chinese astronauts during his preparations for the mission.

Read more: Pakistan-China space mission: Shenzhou-24 pre-launch drill completed

As a payload specialist, Ali’s primary responsibilities aboard the space station will include conducting scientific research, installing experimental equipment and participating in joint scientific experiments between Pakistan and China.

He said the mission would create new opportunities for Pakistan’s scientific and research sectors, particularly through enhanced collaboration in space science and technology.

A Chinese crew member also praised Ali’s preparations for the mission, describing the participation of a Pakistani astronaut as a shared dream between Pakistan and China in the field of space research.

According to the details shared in the briefing, the Shenzhou-24 spacecraft is scheduled to launch from China’s Jiuquan Satellite Launch Centre at 8:18 a.m. China Standard Time.

The mission is expected to mark a significant development in international cooperation at China’s Tiangong Space Station, with Ali set to become its first foreign astronaut visitor.

His participation is being viewed as an important step towards expanding Pakistan’s role in international space research and strengthening scientific ties with China.
