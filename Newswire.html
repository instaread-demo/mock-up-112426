16-year-old student killed after car crashes into bus stop in Kohuwala

November 24, 2026 at 1:04 PM
Listen to audio version of this article

A 16-year-old student was killed and three others were injured in a multi-vehicle accident in Kohuwala on Monday evening (23).

The accident occurred on S. de S. Jayasinghe Mawatha when the driver of a car travelling from Nugegoda towards Kohuwala lost control of the vehicle.

The car initially collided with a motorcycle and another car before striking the student, who was standing at a nearby bus stop.

The vehicle then veered back onto the main road, colliding with another motorcycle, a car and a van.

The student, who sustained serious injuries, died after being admitted to the Kalubowila Hospital.

The deceased was identified as a 16-year-old resident of Nugegoda.

Three others injured in the accident were also admitted to the Kalubowila Hospital (Newswire)
